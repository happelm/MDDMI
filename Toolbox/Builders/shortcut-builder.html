Shortcut builder

Put shortcuts on the Windows desktop or in the Start menu with Intune. Pick where they go, add web links, Edge app windows, programs or network shares, and choose how Intune delivers them: as a remediation that puts deleted or changed shortcuts back, or as a Win32 app with install, uninstall and detection scripts. Icons you upload are embedded in the script, so the device needs no download. The page signs in nowhere and sends nothing: everything stays in your browser.
